What shipped

Plan 71 closed the remaining scraper migration and processing-test gap at e95e426: scheduled listing and detail DAGs, safe claim release, a scraper readiness endpoint, 52 processing integration tests, 95% processing unit coverage, and dashboard visibility into Airflow. This made the orchestrator thin and kept business behavior in tested services.

The surrounding unattributed work completed the same architectural turn. 421f90c replaced dbt-backed operational views with plain Postgres views over HOT tables. 9f08336 rebuilt dbt on DuckDB/MinIO silver, replaced Python integration seeding with declarative dbt unit tests, and overhauled CI. db85b2a and its follow-ups converted the Streamlit dashboard into an analytics product reading DuckDB materializations instead of operational Postgres queries.

Plan 86 added Prometheus and Grafana at 3b84995, with Airflow, Postgres, MinIO, service and host metrics provisioned as code. Later commits added Loki/Promtail aggregation, customer-pipeline gauges, dashboards, Telegram alerts and operational fixes. The plan's purpose was to add observability only after Airflow supplied meaningful production signals to observe.

850cdb9 names Plans 102 and 95. It removed n8n, legacy Postgres and dead service paths while turning /info into a concise portfolio landing page. Follow-ups removed stale tests and mounts, polished the page and demo, and added data-health metrics. Plan 103 appears only in that commit's body after the subject has already attributed the commit to Plans 102 and 95, so it is not an additional attribution under this recap's rule.

The rest of the week repaired issues exposed by the migration: Flyway ordering and grants, SRP scheduling, 403 ownership, cooldown events, archiver foreign keys, VIN collisions, dashboard queries, Caddy/static paths, CI, logging and alert timing. All are named below rather than disappearing for lack of a plan number.

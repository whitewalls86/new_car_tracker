# Completed plans — archive

The single record of what is finished. One row per plan, **newest first**. If
this table and a plan document disagree, the plan document wins and this table
is the bug.

A row arrives here when a plan's last gate closes, and it never leaves. Nothing
else asserts completion: [`PLANS.md`](../PLANS.md) is the index of *active* work
and links here rather than keeping its own copy, which is the duplication
[Plan 146](../plans/plan_146_planning_system.md) removed.

**Dates carry their provenance where they were reconstructed.** Plan 146
Stage 1 walked 134 revisions of `PLANS.md` to recover dates that were never
written down, and an unmarked guess would be manufactured history:

- unmarked — recorded at the time, by whoever closed the plan
- *(observed)* — a dated state transition in `PLANS.md`'s history, or a commit
  whose subject names the plan and carries its implementation
- *(corroborated)* — a date the plan document asserts that git supports
- *(inferred)* — **a guess.** No transition and no asserted date, so the plan
  document's last-touched date stands in

Early rows use a numbering that predates plan documents, including sub-items
(`14.5`) and plans closed together (`54+58`). Plans 0-7 predate plan documents
entirely; nothing can be backfilled for them and they stay as they are.
